# Manbalar va model huquqlari — 7.20

Tekshirilgan: 2026-10-03. Matn va interaktiv sahnalar SinfQuiz uchun qayta yozilgan; manba interfeysi, brendi yoki matni ko‘chirilmagan.

- [Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics 0862](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-lower-secondary/curriculum/mathematics/): son/algebra, geometriya/o‘lchov, statistika/ehtimollik va matematik fikrlash yo‘nalishlari bilan ketma-ketlik solishtirildi. Ochiq dastur tavsifi tekshirildi; yopiq to‘liq framework asosida sertifikatlash da’vosi yo‘q.
- [Common Core — 5.NF](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/5/NF/), [6.EE](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/EE/), [7.G](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/G/), [8.F](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/8/F/): kasr → ifoda/tenglama → geometriya → formula/jadval/grafik aloqasi. Sinf tavsiyasi foydalanuvchini cheklamaydi; dastur xalqaro sertifikatlangan deb taqdim etilmaydi.
- [OpenStax Chemistry 2e — 4.1](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-1-writing-and-balancing-chemical-equations): tenglama koeffitsiyentlari va atomlar saqlanishi. [14.2 pH and pOH](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/14-2-ph-and-poh): pH ta’rifi va haroratga bog‘liq suv ion ko‘paytmasi. [12.2](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/12-2-factors-affecting-reaction-rates): tezlikka ta’sir qiluvchi omillar. O‘quv vizual modeli tajribaviy kinetika ma’lumoti emas.
- [Supabase Auth rate limits](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/rate-limits), [Postgres Changes](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime/postgres-changes): Auth himoyasi hamda RLS bilan boshqariladigan yangilanishlar. Yangi chatning amaliy limitlari loyiha SQLida alohida tekshiriladi.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): klaviatura, fokus, matnli nomlar, kontrast va harakatni kamaytirish. Avtomatik axe tekshiruvi to‘liq WCAG muvofiqlik sertifikati emas.

`MathSolidFallback.jsx`, `math-solid-models.js` va `chemistry-bench-3d.js`dagi geometriya va idishlar original parametrik kod bilan yaratilgan. Yangi tashqi skan yoki sotib olingan 3D aktiv ishlatilmadi. Three.js MIT litsenziyasi npm paketida. Mavjud labirint aktivlari, Kenney va RobotExpressive manbalari/litsenziyalari [MODEL-SOURCES.md](MODEL-SOURCES.md)da; biologiya manbalari [BIOLOGY-SOURCES.md](BIOLOGY-SOURCES.md)da qolgan.
